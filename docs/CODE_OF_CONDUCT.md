# Community Code of Conduct

## Our expectations

DaasaVaani welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels.
Be respectful, patient, and constructive, especially during language,
accessibility, religious-content, and technical discussions.

- Critique ideas and contributions, not people.
- Respect differences in identity, beliefs, language, and ability.
- Credit others' work and respect privacy and intellectual-property rights.
- Accept good-faith feedback and acknowledge mistakes.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexual harassment, doxxing, deliberate
intimidation, and repeated disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies to repository issues, PRs, reviews, discussions, and other
project-managed spaces, including interactions where someone represents the
project. It is a project-specific policy, not an adoption of an external
organization's enforcement process.

## Reporting

For private reporting of abusive content or users, use
[GitHub's reporting abuse guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/acceptable-use-policies/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
and the relevant Report content or Report abuse action.
This sends a report to GitHub, not a confidential project-maintainer inbox.
Do not post private evidence, personal information, or sensitive allegations
publicly. No separate private maintainer conduct contact is currently published.

For routine, non-sensitive moderation requests, ask maintainers to review the
relevant public interaction without repeating abusive material.
Security vulnerabilities follow the [security policy](SECURITY.md) instead.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit/remove contributions, request a behavior change, restrict
participation, or block users in project spaces based on severity and repetition.
They should act proportionately, protect private information, and avoid handling
reports in which they have a personal conflict where another maintainer can act.
GitHub separately enforces its platform policies through its own process.

# Content rights and attribution

The repository's [Apache 2.0 license](../LICENSE) applies to project-authored
software. It does not establish ownership of third-party lyrics, video
descriptions, images, recordings, or trademarks.

## Before import or contribution

- Verify a lawful basis for reproducing and distributing each kind of content,
  including downloadable thumbnails and offline text.
- Record the source video/channel and preserve applicable attribution, notices,
  permissions, and separate license terms with the contribution.
- Do not infer permission from public YouTube availability, a channel link,
  generated transliteration, or the age/traditional character of a composition.
- Respect YouTube API and embedding terms. The product scope does not include
  downloading video media.
- If permission or attribution is unclear, flag it for review and do not publish
  the affected material until resolved.

Source/provenance metadata belongs in reviewed content; exact rights/attribution
field names remain part of the source-schema design. Maintainers must decide
which notices must also accompany generated public and offline artifacts.
Do not store private permission correspondence in public repository files.

## Corrections and rights concerns

For non-sensitive attribution or ownership concerns, open a repository issue with
the affected item/video ID, source, requested correction/removal, and information
you are permitted to share. For confidential evidence, request a private channel
without attaching that evidence publicly. No dedicated private rights-contact
address is currently published.

Maintainers should review concerns promptly when available, consider pausing the
affected publication while rights are unresolved, and coordinate corrections
through source, generated artifacts, and future offline snapshots. Previously
downloaded copies cannot be remotely guaranteed removed.
